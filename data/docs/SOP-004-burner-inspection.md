# SOP-004 Furnace Burner Inspection and Cleaning — FICTIONAL
- Trigger: fuel gas consumption > 5% above expected for the same feed rate for more than 6 h, or uneven flame pattern.
- Inspect burners via peepholes each shift; record flame length and colour.
- Fouled burner tips can be cleaned online one burner at a time with the furnace at reduced firing.
- Keep excess O2 at 2–3%; too low risks incomplete combustion, too high wastes fuel.
- Safety: never open a burner while fuel gas isolation is not confirmed.
