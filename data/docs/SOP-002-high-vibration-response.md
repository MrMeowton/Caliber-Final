# SOP-002 Response to High Vibration on Rotating Equipment — FICTIONAL
| Level | Vibration (mm/s) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | < 4.5 | Routine monitoring |
| Alert | 4.5 – 7.0 | Inform shift supervisor; increase checks to every 2 h; request reliability engineer review within 24 h; check spare-part availability |
| Trip | > 7.0 | Automatic trip; do NOT restart before root cause analysis |
- In the Alert band, also trend bearing temperature. Rising vibration AND temperature together indicates bearing degradation, not a sensor fault.
- A single spike without temperature change is often a probe fault; verify with a portable analyzer.
