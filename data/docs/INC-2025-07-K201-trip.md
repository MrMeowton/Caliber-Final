# Incident Report INC-2025-07 — K-201 Trip — FICTIONAL
- Event: K-201 cracked gas compressor tripped on high vibration (7.8 mm/s) at the drive-end bearing.
- Impact: cracker throughput reduced 40% for 36 h; 2 h of flaring.
- Root cause: bearing degradation from oil contamination. Vibration trend rose for 3 days but stayed below the 7 mm/s alarm.
- Actions: bearing replaced, oil system flushed, monthly oil analysis added.
- Lesson: monitor trends, not only static alarm thresholds.
