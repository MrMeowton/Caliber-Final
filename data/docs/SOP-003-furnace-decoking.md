# SOP-003 Furnace Decoking — FICTIONAL
1. Decoke when tube metal temperature (TMT) > 1,050 °C or coil pressure drop rises > 30% versus clean condition.
2. Isolate naphtha feed; keep dilution steam flowing.
3. Introduce air gradually; keep coil outlet temperature < 850 °C to avoid overheating.
4. Monitor CO2 in effluent; decoking is complete when CO2 < 0.1%.
5. Return the furnace to hot standby before re-introducing feed.
Note: fuel gas intensity typically increases ~0.15% per day after decoking.
