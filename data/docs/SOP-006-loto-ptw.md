# SOP-006 Lockout/Tagout and Permit to Work — FICTIONAL
- Every intervention on process equipment requires a PTW signed by the area authority.
- Isolate all energy sources (electrical, process, hydraulic, stored); apply a personal lock and tag per worker.
- Verify zero energy (try-start, pressure gauge at zero) before work starts.
- Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.
