# Incident Report INC-2026-02 — Flaring after K-401 Trip — FICTIONAL
- Event: K-401 ethylene refrigeration compressor tripped on dry gas seal leak; cold section upset.
- Impact: 6 h of heavy flaring (est. 900 tCO2e), 18 h reduced rate.
- Spare part: dry gas seal cartridge was in stock, which shortened the outage by an estimated 4 months versus ordering.
- Lesson: keeping class-A long-lead spares is justified by downtime cost.
