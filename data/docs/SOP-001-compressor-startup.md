# SOP-001 Compressor Start-up (K-201 / K-301 / K-401) — FICTIONAL
1. Confirm the Permit to Work (PTW) is closed and all LOTO locks are removed by the permit holder (see SOP-006).
2. Check lube oil level > 60% and oil temperature 40–50 °C.
3. Start the auxiliary oil pump; verify oil pressure > 2.5 barg.
4. Open the suction valve slowly; anti-surge valve 100% open.
5. Start the driver and ramp speed gradually to rated speed.
6. Monitor vibration < 4.5 mm/s and bearing temperature < 90 °C. If exceeded, follow SOP-002.
